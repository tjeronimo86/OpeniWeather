# Changelog

## 1.9 – November 6, 2026
- The app is now called OpeniWeather
- New GitHub address: github.com/tjeronimo86/OpeniWeather

## 1.8 – November 5, 2026
- App available in German, English, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Portugal) and Polish
- The app follows your phone's language; Android settings let you pick a language per app
- Place search, weekdays, map and widgets follow the language too
- This changelog appears in German and English
- Updates now download in your browser: the app reports new versions but no longer installs them itself, because Play Protect flagged it for that

## 1.7 – November 5, 2026
- App version with an update button in the location list
- The app checks for updates on its own and reports new versions
- Updates can be downloaded and installed right from the app
- Published version now runs as an optimized release build
- Twilight fixed: sun and moon no longer overlap, and stars only appear once the sky is dark

## 1.6 – November 3, 2026
- Software version at the bottom of the location list
- Link to the latest version on GitHub

## 1.5 – November 3, 2026
- Widget clouds now run smoothly: each frame fades softly into the next instead of jumping
- Clouds always drift in one direction and loop seamlessly, with no more back and forth

## 1.4 – November 3, 2026
- Sky in much higher resolution, with finer cloud detail and better light
- No more flicker when swiping between locations
- Sun and moon no longer slide across the screen as a disc when changing location
- Widgets reanimated: sharper, gently drifting clouds, with rain, snow and lightning as a separate layer
- Cards only fade in the first time they open
- New: this changelog, under "What's New" in the location list

## 1.3 – November 3, 2026
- Widgets show the sky animated

## 1.2 – November 3, 2026
- Five home screen widgets: small, medium, large, daily forecast and sun
- Location choice when editing a widget
- Map fixed: base map, radar, temperature and wind are displayed again

## 1.0 – November 2, 2026
- First version with animated weather sky, hourly and 10-day forecast
- Temperature bars, detail charts and all weather tiles
- Multiple locations, place search and map
